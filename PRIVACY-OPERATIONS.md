## Handling a request

1. Record date received, request type, minimal account/contact reference, owner, deadline and outcome in a private restricted tracker. Do not put customer requests or identity documents in this repository.
2. Assess any need to verify identity proportionately. Use the existing account/email relationship where sufficient; do not routinely ask for passports or bank details.
3. Check applicable deadlines using current ICO guidance. Ordinary rights requests are normally answered within one month; record and explain any lawful verification, clarification or extension. Ordinary support response time is a separate matter.
4. Map the requested action across active app records, Clerk identity, device notification tokens, ad preferences/events and relevant provider records. For erasure, identify legal exceptions and whether store billing continues; account deletion does not cancel a subscription.
5. Execute only the approved request with appropriate verification. Keep minimal confirmation of what was done, any retained categories/reasons and provider follow-up. Current email initiation is not proof that the application can complete deletion; engineering must establish and test the actual workflow before release.
6. Respond through the monitored inbox with the result, any outstanding steps and complaint route. Do not promise instantaneous backup or third-party erasure.

Suggested internal routine, not a founder-confirmed staffing promise: check the inbox each UK working day and review open requests weekly. Agree backup coverage for absence before public app launch.
