## Channel jobs

| Platform | How we market it | Sustainable starting cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram — primary | Strong brand graphics, real app previews and founder Reels. Ask ownership questions in Stories; use the website profile field and Story links. | 3 feed posts/week, including 1 Reel; Stories on 3 days. |
| Facebook — primary | Same original assets with more context. Join a few relevant UK/local rider groups, contribute useful replies, and share only where group rules/admins allow. | 2 Page posts/week; 3 useful community contributions/week. |
| X — secondary | Founder progress, short product clips and specific rider questions. Reply to motorcycle ownership conversations; keep startup jargon out. | 3 posts/week plus a few thoughtful replies. |
| TikTok — two-week test | Founder on camera, ownership frustrations, short actual app demonstrations. Reuse clean vertical footage from Reels with a native caption. | 1–2 videos/week if footage is available. |

Cadences are starting experiments, not algorithm guarantees. Make three core pieces weekly and adapt them across channels. Budget about 2–3 hours/week. Focus outreach on one reachable UK area; choose based on founder access and active communities.

## First two weeks

| Day | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Set matching names, handles, logo, bios and website links. Publish the introduction on Instagram, Facebook and X. |
| 3 | Publish the founder Reel; adapt it for TikTok. Explain the ownership problem and show one working app flow. |
| 5 | Post “Paper folder, phone notes or memory?” as a Story poll and Facebook/X question. Reply to every relevant answer. |
| 8 | Make a short real-app demo: add a maintenance entry. Label it development footage. |
| 10 | Turn a genuine rider answer into a post, anonymously or with permission. Explain how it informs the build. |
| 12 | Show this week’s actual improvement in a short clip. Invite one specific piece of feedback. |
| 14 | Review results and repeat the topic that generated the most relevant rider conversations and profile visits. |

Spend 10 minutes on working days answering comments and contributing to relevant communities. Avoid repetitive promotional replies. Group outreach and publication are proposed founder actions, not actions taken by this kit.

**Provisional two-week success criteria:** publish six core pieces, have ten substantive conversations with UK motorcycle owners, and identify two promising topics. These are work targets, not predicted results. Track reach, profile visits, new followers, saves/shares, relevant comments and link clicks where platform reporting provides them. Website visits/conversion cannot be inferred from social clicks. Confirm the live analytics setup before claiming attribution. Media budget: £0 at this stage.
